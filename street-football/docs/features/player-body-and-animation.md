## What it does now
- The player body is a jointed rig built in code (original, no third-party model): hips, spine, neck and head, arms with shoulder and elbow, legs with hip and knee. Shirt with sleeves, shorts, knee-high socks, boots (color picked per player from a palette), hands, gloves for keepers.
- Face with eyes, nose and ears. Hair styles: bald, buzz, short, afro, long, mohawk, as a cap that follows the head shape, tilted back so the hairline is above the forehead and eyes. Skin and hair colors come from the player profile. Players are scaled to their real height.
- Each limb is a few low-poly shapes merged into one mesh and colored per vertex; all players share one material (about ten draw calls per player).
- Procedural animation (`PlayerAnimator`) writes a `RigPose` every frame: running cycle driven by distance covered (longer strides when faster), knees that fold, opposite arms with bent elbows, hips and shoulders twisting, bob, forward lean; lean with acceleration and into turns; idle stance with breathing; keeper ready stance; when the player moves at an angle to where he faces, the hips turn toward the way he goes and the legs step that way so the feet do not slide.
- Walking animation, blended with the run by speed (`walk_gait_full_speed` 1.7 m/s = pure walk, `run_gait_speed` 2.4 m/s = pure run; `move_blend_full_speed` 1.1; the sprint swing still blends by speed / top sprint speed): smaller hip and arm swing, nearly straight elbows, upright body, body highest in mid-step and lowest with both feet down; small walking touches (`touch_walk_thigh_degrees` 24).
- Pass and kick clips: the actions "pass" and "kick" play recorded joint-angle clips (`data/animations/pass.json`, 16 frames; `kick.json`, 18 frames; 30 fps; right-footed, mirrored for left-footed players by `PoseClips`). `Kicks.kick` plays "pass" for passes (pass, through, lofted, keeper passes) and "kick" for shots and clearances. The lunge toward a far ball is kept. If a clip file is missing, the old coded kick pose is used. The clip starts when the ball leaves the foot; for the coded body there is no wind-up (the Mixamo body has one, see below).
- Short actions (`PlayerActions`): kick and pass (see above), tackle, shoulder charge, keeper dive, skill move, goal celebration, and the dribble touch (the foot swings 0.1 s before the ball is in reach).
- Holding pose: while `Player.ball_in_hands` is true the body holds both arms in front, bent, around the ball (`animation.json` -> `hold_arm_forward`, `hold_arm_out`, `hold_elbow`). It follows the ball in the hands (see `ai.md` for the rules).
- Mixamo body (all players): `ModelBody` loads a skinned model (`assets/art/players/mixamo/soccer_pass.fbx`, Mixamo's "Y Bot" dummy, 65 bones), sized to the player's height and tinted with the kit colour (keepers in the keeper colour, joints darker). `TeamFactory` gives it to every player of both teams: `body.json` -> `model_trial_role` "ALL" (a role = one home player; empty = the coded body for all).
  - Moving plays recorded cycles (since 2026-11-16): `body.json` `model_gaits` = walk (`walk.fbx`, 1.0 m/s, 1.35 m per loop), jog (`jog_forward.fbx`, 2.6 m/s, 2.09 m), run (`run.fbx`, 4.7 m/s, 3.23 m), sprint (`sprint.fbx`, 5.95 m/s, 3.65 m). `ModelBody.advance` picks the two gaits around the player's speed and blends them; the loop advances with the distance covered. Backward: `jog_backward.fbx`. Only standing keeps the coded pose (RigPose) applied to the model's bones. `model_jog_speed` / `model_stride_stretch` are no longer used for forward movement.
  - Chip shots play `chip.fbx` (from 0.46 s, wind 0.17, `chip_seconds` 0.6). A pass received at under `receive_anim_max_speed` 2.0 m/s plays `receive.fbx` (from 0.3 s, `receive_seconds` 0.6; not for a keeper taking it in his hands). Stored, not used yet: `running_to_turn.fbx`, `change_direction.fbx`.
  - Actions play recorded clips on the whole skeleton, from `body.json` -> `model_clips` (file, start seconds, rate, out), blended in and out, keeping only the height of the hips (the player's place stays the game's): pass = `soccer_pass.fbx` from 0.4 s; kick = `strike_forward_jog.fbx` from 0.43 s (keeper: `drop_kick.fbx` from 2.07 s); tackle = `tackle_slide.fbx` ("Soccer Tackle", the long slide) from 0.45 s at rate 1.3, out 0.8 (`tackle_seconds` 0.9; played on every TACKLE, also with nobody on the ball, and for AI tackles; no forward lunge added); dive = `diving_save.fbx` from 1.0 s, mirrored for the other side; keeper catch = `keeper_catch.fbx` from 1.2 s; keeper scoop = `keeper_scoop.fbx` from 0.6 s (ball below `keeper_scoop_height` 0.5 m), both played by `KeeperHands.take`; keeper place = `keeper_place.fbx` from 0.6 s, played by `KeeperHands.drop`. Clips can be mirrored (left-footed players, dive side); each animation is loaded once for all players.
  - Wind-up (user's choice, option 1 of 2; the other, delaying the ball to play the whole clip, was not chosen): while a kick is held (power bar shown: PASS / THROUGH held, SHOOT / CLEAR charging; AI shots too) the model's body goes to the backswing of the clip, `wind` seconds before its contact (`model_clips`: pass 0.17, kick 0.17, keeper kick 0.2), fully from `model_windup_full` 0.35 of the bar, with weight `model_windup_weight` 0.85. The strike plays on release; a quick tap shows only the strike. `Player.update_look` sets `PlayerAnimator.windup` / `windup_amount` / `windup_side`; `ModelBody.apply(..., windup, windup_amount)`.
  - Tackle clips (since 2026-11-16, replacing `tackle_slide.fbx`, removed): "tackle_run" = `running_slide.fbx` from 0.2 s (`tackle_run_seconds` 1.1), "tackle" = `tackle_quick.fbx` ("Soccer Tackle") from 0.05 s (`tackle_seconds` 1.2); the text on the tackle clip above is out of date. Rules in `defending.md`.
  - Costs: about 49,000 triangles per player; bone posing in script about 0.2 ms per player per frame on CI (headless AI match test 45 s -> 145 s); APK 31 -> 41 MB. Frame rate on the phone not checked yet.
  - Limits: no face, hair or kit on the dummy; the dribble touch no longer lines the foot up with the ball while running. Missing clips (asked from the user): idle, walk, sprint, dribble, header, celebration, keeper idle, throw.

## How to add a new clip
`python3 tools/mixamo_to_clip.py file.fbx data/animations/name.json [before] [after]` (binary FBX, needs numpy). It keeps joint angles only (RigPose's 22 channels), cuts from just before the contact frame (the fastest foot) to `after` seconds after it, and mirrors to right-footed. Then play it with `PoseClips` from `PlayerActions`. List the source in `README.md` (asset licenses). The FBX files for the clips were first not committed; the two used by the Mixamo body now are (see History).

## Do not undo
- Limbs are merged primitive shapes colored per vertex with ONE shared material: about ten draw calls per player. No custom shaders: the phone rejected custom shaders in the other game, and emulators accept things real phones reject.
- The head and hair use a finer mesh (`head_segments` 12). The hair is a cap thicker than the head, tilted back 35 degrees (`hair_tilt_degrees`): with the first coarse 8-segment version the head showed through the hair. The hairline must stay above the eyes.
- Body and animation are look only: no gameplay effect. All sizes stay in `body.json`, all animation numbers in `animation.json`.
- Procedural animation, no rig files: assets stay original and small, every number tunable.
- Cartoon style is kept (the user said so after the video comparison). Do not push toward realism without asking.
- Check the look with the motion study images, not by guessing.

- Clips for the coded body are joint angles only, from the user's own Mixamo downloads (free for use in games). The Mixamo body trial put the raw FBX files and the dummy in the repo (public; Adobe does not allow redistributing them on their own): this replaces the earlier "no FBX in the repo" rule, and the user knows. 3D models with a real club kit or a real player's likeness were refused: fictional content only, public repo.
