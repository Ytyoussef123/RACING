## What it does now
- Power bar red end (step 1, `feature/kick-power`): SHOOT is unchanged (it fills the bar from the press over `shot_charge_time` 0.9 s; a quick tap is a soft shot). The bar now has a red end from `kick_red_zone` 0.85 (a red zone at the end of the bar, the fill turns red inside it; `ui.json` -> `power_red_zone`, `power_fill_red`). In the red end a shot rises more by chance: up to `shot_red_lift` 4.5 m/s extra at full power, times the shooter's finishing error over `shot_red_reference_degrees` 3 (clamped `shot_red_scale_min` 0.4 to `shot_red_scale_max` 1.6, so better finishers rise less). It can fly over the crossbar; before, no shot could (a full-power shot rose at most about 1.65 m, the bar is 2 m). The AI charges shots to at most 0.75, below the red end, so balance is unchanged (3.67 goals, 31.5 shots, 16.0 saves).
- CLEAR (SHOOT in our own half) kicks on release: a tap keeps the automatic clearance (speed 17, lift 6.5); held, the bar sets `clear_power_speed_min/max` 10 to 20 m/s and `clear_power_lift_min/max` 4 to 7.5 m/s; the red end gives up to x1.6 aim error (`kick_red_error_mult`). Bar shows only after `kick_tap_seconds` 0.18 s.
- Shot types (step 3, `feature/shot-types`; user decisions 2026-11-15): the bar sets only the power, a slide on SHOOT sets the type; no type label next to the bar. Press SHOOT, slide the finger (more up/down than sideways = chip/low; more sideways = curl; at least `button_slide_fraction` 0.5 of the radius), release. The slide is read while held and as the finger is lifted; the type read during the hold is kept for the release. Keys on a PC: V chip, B curled shot, N low shot (each also works as SHOOT; hold for power). Clearances (SHOOT in our own half) ignore the slide.
  - Normal shot (no slide): unchanged (speed 12-26 m/s, lift 0.5-5.5, red end can go over the bar).
  - Low shot (slide down; `ShotTypes.low`): along the ground (no lift), speed `shot_low_speed_min` 11 to `shot_low_speed_max` 24 m/s by the bar, never over the bar; in the red end more aim error (x up to 1.6).
  - Chip (slide up; `ShotTypes.chip`): a lob aimed at the `ShotAim` point, coming down `shot_chip_distance_min` 4 to `shot_chip_distance_max` 24 m away by the bar (after the finisher adjustment), apex `shot_chip_apex_base` 2.6 + 0.06 per meter (clamped 2.8 to 4.2 m), well above a keeper's reach (test: 3.3 m). Its ideal power brings it through the goal line at `shot_chip_cross_height` 1.2 m from any distance (closer to the line for a short chip). Too little power: it comes down short; right: drops under the bar; too much: over the bar.
  - Curled shot (slide left or right; `ShotTypes.curl`): to `shot_curl_post_inset` 0.5 m inside the far post at `shot_curl_height` 1.3 m. The far post is the post opposite the shooter; from the middle (within `shot_curl_center_band` 1 m) the post away from the keeper. Spin `shot_curl_spin` 0.8 bends it back toward the middle; the launch direction is found on simulated flights so that, at the ideal power, it starts wide of the far post and curls back inside it. Ideal power grows with the distance: `shot_curl_ideal_base` 0.3 + 0.025 per meter, clamped 0.35 to 0.85. Speed `shot_curl_speed_min` 12 to `shot_curl_speed_max` 24 m/s; the lift changes `shot_curl_lift_per_power` 6 m/s per unit of power away from the ideal. So: right power in the corner; a bit too much faster, less curl and higher (post or bar); too much over the bar (the red end adds lift); too little slower, more curl, into the middle where the keeper is. Aim error x `shot_curl_error_mult` 0.7.
  - Good-power zone by the player (`ShotTypes.skilled`): the power the shot really gets is ideal + (bar - ideal) x the finisher's error over `shot_red_reference_degrees` 3, clamped `shot_skill_damp_min` 0.5 to `shot_skill_damp_max` 1.3: a good finisher's mistake counts half, a poor one's 1.3 times. Used by the chip and the curl.
  - Keeper saves now depend on the shot (`KeeperSave`, see `ai.md`); `Possession.last_shot_type` keeps the last shot's type.
  - Later (user): Blitz shot, volleys, penalties.
- Aim (`scripts/gameplay/shot_aim.gd`, `ShotAim.target`): every shot is aimed at a point on the opponent's goal line. A stick pointing at or near the goal keeps its aim (it can still miss up to `shot_assist_margin` 1.5 m past a post); a stick pointing elsewhere aims `shot_post_inset` 0.45 m inside the post on that side; no stick aims at the corner away from the keeper. The point is computed when SHOOT is released (a queued shot keeps that point); the shot direction goes from the ball to it.
- The error still decides if it is on target: finishing, long-shot distance, weak foot, speed mode, first-time skill, plus a tight angle (goal seen under `shot_tight_angle_degrees` 20 degrees: error up to x`shot_tight_error_mult` 1.6) and the back to the goal (shot more than 90 degrees from the facing: up to x`shot_turn_error_mult` 1.5).
- After any kick the kicker's body lets the ball through for `kick_pass_through_seconds` 0.15 s (`player.ghost_timer`).
- In our own half SHOOT becomes CLEAR (see `touch-controls-and-hud.md`).
- The AI's aim already crosses the goal, so its shots are aimed as before.
- Planned, not done: a "Blitz" shot (a curling power shot from the reference game), asked by the user for later.
- SHOOT: hold to charge (`shot_charge_time` 0.9 s, power bar above the player), release to shoot. Speed between `shot_speed_min` 12 and `shot_speed_max` 26 m/s, lift between 0.5 and 5.5. Turning while shooting curls the ball (`shot_curve_from_turn`).
- Stats: shot power scales the speed (`shot_speed_mult`); finishing sets the aim error (`shot_error_degrees`); long shots add error per meter beyond `long_shot_distance` 12 m (`long_shot_error_per_meter`).
- Speed mode: the aim error is multiplied by x0.85 walking, x1.15 sprinting, x1.2 in a knock-on, for everyone, AI too (`player.json` -> `pace_error_mult_walk`, `pace_error_mult_sprint`, `pace_error_mult_knock_on`; `BallHandling._pace_mult`).
- Weak foot: a kick aimed well to the weak side (more than `weak_side_degrees` 35 off the facing) has more error, by weak-foot stars (`weak_foot_error_mult` [2.6, 2.0, 1.5, 1.15] for 1 to 4 stars).
- Special skills: `first_time_shot` makes a one-touch shot (the shooter does not hold the ball) more accurate (error x0.6, `skills.json` -> `first_time_shot_error_mult`); `rabona` removes the weak-foot penalty.
- A shot needs the ball within `kick_reach`; a shot asked early is queued (kick buffer) and played when the ball is reached; the aim is chosen at the press.
- On defense the SHOOT button becomes TACKLE; the earlier version (PRESS on this button) was replaced (see `defending.md`).
- The AI shoots in range (aims at the corner away from the keeper with a difficulty-based error). Match stats count shots.

## Do not undo
- Hold to charge and release: power rises with the charge.
- Every accuracy and power effect goes through the stat sheet and `stat_effects.json`; weak foot error grows as stars fall.
- `first_time_shot` and `rabona` effects stay tied to `special_skills` (check `ball_handling.gd`).
- Kicks only with the ball in reach; early requests are queued, not played from a distance.
- AI aim tuning (inside the post, skipped bad-angle shots) came from a measured balance problem; check `MATCH BALANCE` after changing shot numbers.

- After any kick the kicker's body must let the ball through for `kick_pass_through_seconds` 0.15 s (`player.ghost_timer`). Without it a shot or pass played back across his own body bounces off his collision circle (bug found by "shot on target: running along the goal line, the shot heads on goal").
- Every shot is aimed at the goal; a shot must not miss only because the shooter is not facing the goal (user request). The error decides the rest.

- A held button keeps the meaning of its press until the finger is lifted (`HumanInput._pressed_as`); do not bring back `_shoot_locked` / `_was_attacking`.
- The AI keeps shot charge at most 0.75 (below the red end) so balance is not changed by the bar.
- The bar sets only the power (user decision, 2026-11-15); the slide on SHOOT sets the type. Any side slide curls toward the far post; no type label next to the bar (user decisions).
- The keeper's roll is the only save: a beaten keeper is ghosted 0.3 s; without it his body blocked the ball as a hidden save and the save numbers were meaningless. No save once the ball is behind him.
- The AI keeps its shots below the red end. Measure balance with `MATCH SHOT TYPES` as well as `MATCH BALANCE`.
