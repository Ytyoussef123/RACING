## What it does now
- Lofted pass and lofted through ball (step 2, `feature/lofted-pass`):
  - Gesture: press PASS or THROUGH, slide the finger up on the button (more up than sideways, at least `button_slide_fraction` 0.5 of the button radius, `controls.json`), release: the ball goes in the air. The slide is read while the finger is down and as it is lifted (a slide made just before lifting counts). Keyboard for PC testing: T = lofted pass, G = lofted through ball (hold for power). User words: "the ball doesn't go on the ground, it makes an arc", for example to switch the play from one side of the pitch to the other.
  - Power: a lofted kick always takes the power bar's power, filled from the press (no tap time; `kick_charge_time` 0.6 s for a full bar); the bar shows at once. The ball comes down `lofted_distance_min` 5 m to `lofted_distance_max` 30 m away by the bar (`LoftedKick.distance_for` / `power_for`). Too little power: it falls short; right: it lands at the teammate; too much: over his head. The distance comes only from the bar ("the long ball depends only on the power").
  - Direction: toward the teammate on the stick's side (the same pass assist as PASS), aimed where he will be when it comes down (his velocity x flight time x `pass_lead_factor` 0.8). A lofted through ball goes to the space in front of him (`PassTargeting.through_spot`, the through pass's spot, now shared). With nobody on that side: along the stick. If no power is given (AUTO), it lands at the teammate.
  - Flight (`scripts/gameplay/lofted_kick.gd`, `LoftedKick`): apex = `lofted_apex_base` 1.8 + `lofted_apex_per_meter` 0.1 per meter, clamped `lofted_apex_min` 2.2 to `lofted_apex_max` 4.0 m, over the heads of players in between (test: 3.3 m high over a defender half way on a 16 m ball). The speed is corrected once on a quick simulated flight (air drag), so it lands within a meter of the distance asked (test).
  - Accuracy: the `lofted_pass` stat (`through_error_degrees`), weak foot, speed mode, red end of the bar (x up to 1.6).
  - Air control (`scripts/gameplay/air_control.gd`, `AirControl`): a ball coming down (falling faster than `air_control_min_drop_speed` 0.5 m/s) can be taken up to `air_control_height` 1.5 m (chest, thigh or foot) instead of only near the ground (`control_max_ball_height` 0.5-0.8 by the jump stat), by either side, so a defender under the ball can win it. One first touch per player per kick: clean with `air_control_chance` (stat effect from ball control: 0.55 to 0.95; fallback 0.8 for players without a profile) minus `air_control_speed_penalty` 0.03 per m/s above `air_control_easy_speed` 9 (not below `air_control_chance_min` 0.2). Missed: a heavy touch, the ball bounces off him away from him (`air_heavy_touch_speed` 3.5 m/s, `air_heavy_touch_lift` 1.5, `air_heavy_touch_spread` 0.6) and he cannot retake it for `air_heavy_touch_block` 0.4 s. It applies to every ball coming down (lofted passes, clearances, long kicks, high bounces).  - Keeper with the ball in his hands: a slide is ignored, PASS stays the throw and THROUGH the long kick.
  - AI: see `ai.md` (`LoftedAI`).
  - Code moved: the held-button logic (tap / hold / bar / lofted) moved from `BallHandling` into `scripts/gameplay/held_kick.gd` (`HeldKick`); `BallHandling` is now 264 lines and does the air first touch in `_try_take`. `Kicks.lofted` is the new kick. `PlayerIntent`: `pass_lofted`, `through_lofted` (human slide or AI choice), `kick_power` (AI power, -1 = automatic).
- Power bar (step 1, `feature/kick-power`): PASS and THROUGH kick on RELEASE. A tap (held less than `kick_tap_seconds` 0.18 s) keeps the automatic power described below, exactly as before. Held longer, the bar fills over `kick_charge_time` 0.6 s and sets the power:
  - pass: speed `pass_power_speed_min` 6 to `pass_power_speed_max` 20 m/s (automatic: 6 + 0.55 per m, 7 to 16); to nobody: same range (automatic `pass_speed` 11).
  - through pass: lead in front of the runner `through_power_lead_min` 2 to `through_power_lead_max` 10 m (automatic `through_lead_distance` 4), times the vision stat; to nobody: `through_power_speed_min` 7 to `through_power_speed_max` 17 m/s (automatic 10).
  - keeper throw (PASS): `keeper_throw_power_speed_min` 6 to `keeper_throw_power_speed_max` 16 m/s. Keeper long kick (THROUGH): lands `keeper_long_kick_power_distance_min` 10 to `keeper_long_kick_power_distance_max` 32 m away toward the teammate (or along the aim), lift 7.
  - Red end from `kick_red_zone` 0.85: a pass or through pass gets up to `kick_red_error_mult` 1.6 times its aim error. The bar only sets the power.
  - The AI is unchanged: it presses PASS/THROUGH for one frame (automatic power).
- PASS (tap): the ball goes to the teammate on the joystick's side, even if he is not exactly on the line (pass assist). Cone 70 degrees, scored mostly by angle, small penalty for a blocked lane. If nobody is in the cone, the teammate closest to the joystick direction is chosen, up to 120 degrees; beyond that, no pass. The ball goes to where the receiver is running (`pass_lead_factor`).
- THROUGH: ball into the space `through_lead_distance` (4 m) in front of the receiver, mostly toward the opponent goal and partly along his run, kept inside the court.
- Pass speed grows with distance (`pass_speed_base` + `pass_speed_per_meter`, between min and max). Error depends on low pass (PASS) and lofted pass (THROUGH) stats; vision changes the through-pass lead.
- Speed mode: the error is multiplied by x0.85 walking, x1.15 sprinting, x1.2 in a knock-on, for everyone, AI too (`player.json` -> `pace_error_mult_walk`, `pace_error_mult_sprint`, `pace_error_mult_knock_on`; `KickAccuracy.pace_mult`).
- Kick accuracy (`with_error`, `weak_foot_mult`, `pace_mult`, `shot_angle_mult`) lives in `scripts/gameplay/kick_accuracy.gd` (`KickAccuracy`), moved out of `BallHandling`.
- The kicked ball passes through the kicker's body for `kick_pass_through_seconds` 0.15 s (`player.ghost_timer`), also for passes played across the body.
- Receive assist: the pass target runs to the earliest point of the predicted ball path (`BallPhysics.copy()` stepped with walls, 2 s ahead) that he can reach in time, and waits there. The joystick keeps aiming (`PlayerIntent.aim`), so first-time kicks go where you point (forward if the stick is neutral). AI receivers and loose-ball chasers use the same prediction.
- Kicks are only played with the ball within reach (`kick_reach` 1.1 m, ball not higher than `kick_max_ball_height`). A pass, through pass or shot asked a little early is queued (kick buffer, `kick_buffer_seconds` 0.5 s, or 1.2 s while dribbling with the aim chosen at the press) and played when the ball is reached. No kicking from a distance.
- Control switches to the receiver when a pass leaves for a teammate. A pass deflected by the other team stops being a pass (`Possession.touched`).
- On defense the PASS button becomes SWITCH (see `defending.md`).
- Keeper with the ball in his HANDS (rules in `ai.md`):
  - PASS = throw: pass speed by distance, plus `keeper_throw_lift` 1.5, error x `keeper_throw_error_mult` 0.6 (`KeeperHands.throw_velocity`).
  - THROUGH = long kick: lift `keeper_long_kick_lift` 7 m/s, speed chosen so the ball lands `keeper_long_kick_landing` 0.9 of the way to the teammate, between `keeper_long_kick_speed_min` 8 and `keeper_long_kick_speed_max` 20 m/s; with no teammate, `keeper_long_kick_space_speed` 16 m/s into space (`KeeperHands.long_kick_velocity`).
  - SHOOT = clear (the existing CLEAR).
  - A kick from the hands is always allowed (`_can_kick` is true for a ball in the hands) and clears `ball_in_hands` (`_kick`).
- Back-pass rule: a ball he receives from a teammate's pass can only be played with his feet (`KeeperHands.may_handle` is false when the last kick was a "pass" by a teammate). Other balls in his area (shots, clearances, loose balls) can be caught.

## Do not undo
- The pass goes to the teammate on the joystick's side even if he is not exactly on the line (pass assist). The user called this an important point and said "for the pass, it's good now, I like it". Do not narrow the cone or the fallback without asking.
- The receiver gets a receive assist (user request): he runs to the predicted meeting point while the joystick only aims. Without it the stick could push him away from the ball.
- Kicks are only played with the ball within reach: a pass, through pass or shot asked a little early is queued (kick buffer) and played when the ball is reached. No kicking from a distance.
- A pass deflected by the other team stops being a pass (`Possession.touched`).

- The kicked ball passes through the kicker for `kick_pass_through_seconds` 0.15 s; without it a pass across his own body bounces off him.
- Back-pass rule: a teammate's pass to the keeper is played with his feet, never caught (user decision, 2026-11-15).

- A tap on PASS or THROUGH keeps the automatic power (user decision, 2026-11-15); only a hold lets the bar decide, and the bar sets only the power. The AI keeps the automatic power, so balance is not changed by the bar.
- A held button keeps the meaning of its press until the finger is lifted (`HumanInput._pressed_as`).
- A lofted kick's distance comes only from the power bar (user decision, 2026-11-15). Its direction is toward the teammate on the stick's side (user decision).
- The slide is read at release too (a slide made just before lifting counts). One air first touch per player per kick: no rolling the dice every frame.
