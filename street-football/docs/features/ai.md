## What it does now
- Every player, human or AI, is driven by the same `PlayerIntent`. The AI has no cheating inputs.
- Team brain: picks a chaser (closest outfield player) and does greedy marking in its half. Off-ball players hold formation slots that shift with the ball and step away from defenders to get open.
- Ball carrier: shoots in range (aims at the corner away from the keeper, inset from the post), passes when pressed or when a teammate is open further up with a clear lane, else dribbles around defenders. Uses skill moves when a defender is within 2 m.
- Shot choice: skips shots at a goal angle under 9 degrees or through a defender, unless within 6 m.
- Receivers and loose-ball chasers use the same intercept prediction as the human receive assist.
- Keeper: stays between ball and goal, moves across for shots, rushes loose balls near goal, saves once per shot with a difficulty-based chance. Goalkeepers protect the goal automatically, also on the human's team; the human only controls his keeper when the keeper has the ball (see `match-flow.md`).
- Keeper with the ball (main description of the goalkeeper-with-the-ball feature, same rules for both keepers):
  - He catches (ball in his HANDS) only inside his area (`KeeperHands.in_area`); outside it he parries or blocks the ball away (`keeper_parry_back`, `keeper_parry_side`, `keeper_parry_block_seconds`).
  - A catch in the area is a ball in the hands unless it is a back-pass (the last kick was a "pass" by a teammate): then he plays it with his feet. `KeeperHands.may_handle` decides.
  - In the hands he cannot leave the area (`KeeperHands.clamp_to_area`). The ball is held at `keeper_hands_height` 1.0 m, `keeper_hands_forward` 0.3 m in front, never closer than `keeper_hands_line_margin` 0.3 m to his own goal line. A goal kick starts with the ball in his hands.
  - `KeeperAI._distribute`: from the hands, a throw to a teammate within `keeper_throw_max_distance` 14 m, else a long kick; with the feet (after a back-pass) a normal pass. `KeeperAI.best_outlet` and `outlet_direction` are public (the hands limit uses them). After `keeper_hands_seconds` 6 in the hands he throws to the best outlet automatically.
  - Throw and long kick numbers and formulas: see `passing-and-receiving.md`.
  - Ball at his feet: he plays like a normal outfield player and can be tackled anywhere (see `defending.md`).
- Difficulties easy / normal / hard (decision interval, speed factor, shot error, shoot range, pass tendency, tackle chances, keeper save chance, skill tendency). The AI's aim error is added to the player's own stat-based error. Decisions run at the difficulty's interval, movement runs every frame.
- `shoot_range` is 8 / 9.5 / 11 m for easy / normal / hard (was 9 / 11 / 12.5).
- The speed modes apply to the AI too: sprinting with the ball keeps it close, and accuracy changes by mode. The AI does not walk or knock-on yet (planned after the user's controls test).
- Keeper save chance is 0.4 / 0.6 / 0.68 (easy / normal / hard; hard was 0.75), then multiplied by the shot (see the next lines).
- Keeper saves (shot types, `scripts/ai/keeper_save.gd`, `KeeperSave.chance`, one roll when the shot comes within his reach): difficulty chance x reflexes x placement (`keeper_save_center_mult` 1.65 at him to `keeper_save_corner_mult` 0.85 at the edge of his reach, from where the ball will pass him) x speed (`keeper_save_slow_mult` 1.1 at 10 m/s to `keeper_save_fast_mult` 0.9 at 26 m/s) x `keeper_save_high_mult` 0.75 above `keeper_save_high_height` 1.6 m or `keeper_save_low_mult` 0.85 below `keeper_save_low_height` 0.4 m x (1 - `keeper_save_spin_penalty` 0.45 x spin); clamped `keeper_save_min_chance` 0.03 to 0.97. A hard shot is still parried (rebound) above the catch speed. A ball already behind the keeper (`keeper_behind_margin` 0.3 m; a chip over his head) cannot be saved. A beaten keeper (failed roll) is ghosted for `keeper_beaten_ghost_seconds` 0.3 s: the ball goes past him; his body no longer blocks it as a hidden second save.
- Keeper coming out: as an opponent with the ball closes in he leaves his line, from `keeper_out_start_distance` 8 m to `keeper_out_full_distance` 4 m from the goal center, up to `keeper_out_max` 1.8 m (line offset 1 m otherwise; never more than half the distance to the ball).
- AI shot types (`scripts/ai/ai_shots.gd`, `AiShots`: `choose`, `charge`): a chip when the keeper is `ai_chip_keeper_out` 1.4 m or more off his line and the goal is `ai_chip_min_distance` 5 to `ai_chip_max_distance` 15 m away (`chip_tendency` 0.1 / 0.2 / 0.3); a curled shot from `ai_curl_min_distance` 8 to `ai_curl_max_distance` 16 m (`curl_tendency` 0.1 / 0.2 / 0.3); a low shot within `ai_low_max_distance` 8 m (`low_tendency` 0.2 / 0.25 / 0.3); otherwise the normal shot. A chip or curl gets its ideal power give or take `shot_power_error` (0.15 / 0.08 / 0.04), kept below the red end. `AiPlayerState.shot_type`; `CarrierAI` sets `intent.shot_type` while it holds SHOOT. `AiTuning` has `chip_tendency`, `curl_tendency`, `low_tendency`, `shot_power_error`.
- The AI's shot aim already crosses the goal, so the human shot-on-target aim (`ShotAim`) changed nothing for it.
- Team movement (details in `team-movement.md`): with the ball, off-ball outfield players get a job (support, forward, cover) and keep about 5 m apart; the forward makes runs in behind, the carrier plays through passes to them, the passer does a give-and-go. Without the ball: one presses, one covers behind the ball, others mark (most advanced free opponents, closest pair first); defenders keep 3 m apart. Run, through-pass and give-and-go chances change with difficulty.
- Lofted pass (`scripts/ai/lofted_ai.gd`, `LoftedAI`): when the carrier decides to pass but no ground pass is on (`best_pass` finds nobody), it may play a ball in the air (switch of play) to a teammate `lofted_ai_min_distance` 9 to `lofted_ai_max_distance` 26 m away with no opponent within `lofted_ai_open_distance` 3.5 m of him, not more than `lofted_ai_max_back_frac` 0.1 of the court further back; the most open and most advanced one. Chance per decision `lofted_tendency` (easy 0.2, normal 0.35, hard 0.5). Its power is the one for where the teammate will be, plus or minus `lofted_power_error` (easy 0.12, normal 0.07, hard 0.04 of the bar). It sets `PlayerIntent.pass_lofted` and `kick_power`. `AiTuning` has `lofted_tendency` and `lofted_power_error`. Flight and landing: see `passing-and-receiving.md`.
- After a pass (`scripts/ai/after_pass.gd`, `AfterPass`): `Kicks.kick` calls `AfterPass.start(player)` after a pass (not for keepers); `TeamBrain.update` calls `AfterPass.adjust` on the intent of every AI-driven outfield player (including the user's former player once control has switched to the receiver). For `after_pass_settle_seconds` 0.25 he stands still facing the ball; then for `after_pass_jog_seconds` 1.2 he does not sprint and moves at most `after_pass_jog_speed` 0.7 of his run speed, facing the ball while moving away from it (he jogs backward, the Jog Backward clip plays), forward otherwise. A give-and-go or a run in behind starts right after the short stop, at running pace (no sprint) until the jog time is over (since 2026-11-16). The other side winning the ball, or him getting the ball, ends it at once. State: `AiPlayerState.after_pass`. `PlayerIntent.face` carries where the body looks (see `player-movement.md`).
- AI tackle (since 2026-11-16): a standing tackle (`PlayerIntent.tackle_slide` false), decided at once; it slides only with `ai.json` `slide_tackle_chance` 0.15 (details in `defending.md`).
- Sprinting: the AI decides alone; it sprints when its target is more than 6 m away or when chasing the carrier. A marker follows the user's player, so it sprints when they do. Both sides have the same top speeds apart from the difficulty `speed_factor` and each player's speed stat (explained to the user, 2026-11-16, not changed; offered: markers reacting with a delay, or sprinting less to save energy; the user decides).
- Carrier pass weights are now in `ai.json` (`pass_progress_weight`, `pass_open_weight`, `pass_open_cap`, `pass_blocked_score`), same values.
- The human's teammates use "normal" (`teammates_difficulty`). The default opponent formation is 1-2-1.
