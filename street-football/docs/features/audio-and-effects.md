# Audio and visual effects

Last change: 2026-11-14, Phase 6 part 1: animation, effects, sound and music (branch `feature/polish`, commit `cff05f4`)

## What it does now
- All sounds are original, generated by `tools/generate_audio.py` (numpy synthesis to WAV, no samples): soft and hard kicks, ground bounce, wall rattle, post ping, net swish, short and full-time whistles, goal horn, tackle scuff, keeper save, UI click, plus a 21-second 90 BPM street beat (A minor).
- Volume follows the impact strength (event speed against `full_at`); pitch varies a little so repeats differ.
- Music plays in the menus; not in the match by default (`music_in_match` false). Sound and Music can be turned off in Settings.
- Effects: ball trail on fast balls, dust puffs on won tackles and shoulder duels, confetti in the scorer's colors at a goal, camera shake on goals and post hits, GOAL! text pop. The ball is drawn 1.5x bigger than its physics size (look only).
- Events reach sounds and effects through the match events channel (`ctx.emit_event`): pass, shot, ground, wall, post, net, tackle_won, shoulder_won, save, skill.

## Where it lives
- Code: `scripts/core/sfx.gd` (Sfx autoload): `play`, `play_event(kind, strength)`, `want_music`, `refresh_music`; pool of players.
- Code: `scripts/gameplay/effects.gd` (Effects): `dust`, `confetti` (particle bursts).
- Code: `scripts/gameplay/match_context.gd`: `emit_event(kind, at, strength, label)`; events sent from `ball_handling.gd`, `tackle_rules.gd`, `shoulder_charge.gd`, `keeper_ai.gd`, `skill_moves.gd`, `match.gd`.
- Code: `scripts/gameplay/match_camera.gd` (MatchCamera): `shake(strength, seconds)`.
- Tool: `tools/generate_audio.py`; output `assets/audio/*.wav`.
- Numbers: `data/config/audio.json` -> `sounds` (file, `volume_db`, `pitch_jitter`, `full_at` for each sound), `event_sounds` (event -> sound), `min_ground_bounce_speed` 2.0, `min_volume` 0.15, `music_file`, `music_volume_db` -12, `music_in_match` false, `pool_size` 8.
- Numbers: `data/config/animation.json` -> effects: `ball_trail_min_speed` 14, `ball_visual_scale` 1.5, `goal_shake_strength` 0.35, `goal_shake_seconds` 0.5, `hit_shake_strength` 0.12, `hit_shake_seconds` 0.2, `message_pop_scale` 1.6, `message_pop_seconds` 0.35.
- Tests: `tests/ui_flow_test.gd` (sound and music settings); look checked on the screenshots (`tests/screenshot_tour.gd`). No automatic test of the sound itself.

## Do not undo
- All sounds are original and generated by `tools/generate_audio.py`; they are listed in the README asset licenses. Reason: no license issue. Add any new sound there and in the README table.
- Events reach sounds and effects through `ctx.emit_event`, so gameplay code does not call them directly.
- The ball is drawn 1.5x bigger than its physics size: reads better on a phone; physics unchanged.
- Music is off in the match by default (menus only).
- Camera shake and message pop numbers stay in `animation.json`.
- Effect numbers are look only: they must not change gameplay.

## How to check after a change
- CI: all test suites pass; no script errors (`audio.json` file paths wrong would show as errors).
- Screenshots: ball size, effects visible in the tour.
- Phone test: kicks, bounces, wall rattle, whistles, goal horn, volume; Sound and Music switches.
- After changing `tools/generate_audio.py`, run `python3 tools/generate_audio.py` and commit the new WAV files.
