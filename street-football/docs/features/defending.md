## What it does now
- When your team does not hold the ball (a loose ball counts, unless it is our own pass on its way to a teammate), the four buttons switch to the defense layout: THROUGH -> MATCH-UP, SHOOT -> TACKLE, PASS -> SWITCH, SPRINT -> PRESS (eFootball-style "Dash & Pressure"). Keyboard: R, Space, E (switch: Q), Shift.
- PRESS (hold SPRINT on defense): the selected player goes to take the ball by himself: sprints goal-side of the carrier, then to the ball. It does NOT tackle by itself: the tackle is the user's TACKLE button (`player.json` `press_auto_tackle` 0; 1 brings the automatic tackle back). On a loose ball he sprints to where he can meet it.
- PRESS with the joystick moved (since 2026-11-17): the player follows the stick but feels "a little difficult" to pull away. `PressAssist.pull`: move = (1 - k) x stick + k x direction of the press (the carrier, or the intercept point of a loose ball), k = `player.json` `press_stick_pull` 0.25; sprint and stick length kept. With the stick released, PRESS alone is the automatic press above (he runs back to the carrier; this already worked). Before 2026-11-17 the stick moved case was a plain sprint where the stick points (user decision 2026-11-15). The code is in `scripts/gameplay/human_assists.gd` (`HumanAssists`), out of `match.gd`.
- MATCH-UP (hold): shadows the carrier goal-side at `match_up_contain_distance` (1.6 m), follows his moves, no auto tackle (tap TACKLE).
- TACKLE is a real slide (since 2026-11-16; `TackleRules.attempt`, `slide_update`, `_resolve`; `Player.start_slide`, `slide_time`, `tackle_pending`). At `slide_run_speed` 2.5 m/s or more the action is "tackle_run" (`running_slide.fbx` from 0.2 s, `tackle_run_seconds` 1.1), below it "tackle" (`tackle_quick.fbx`, the "Soccer Tackle" clip, from 0.05 s, `tackle_seconds` 1.2). The player slides along his way for `slide_seconds` 0.8, from `slide_speed_mult` 1.2 x his speed (at least `slide_min_speed` 4.5 m/s) down to a stop, and cannot steer. The slide plays even with nobody to tackle. AI tackles follow the same rule. The old long clip `tackle_slide.fbx` was removed.
- Slide or standing (since 2026-11-16): `PlayerIntent.tackle_slide` and `TackleRules.attempt(tackler, ctx, slide)`. The human's TACKLE is always a slide (`MatchUpAssist` passes `tackle_slide` on). The AI makes a standing tackle: decided at once (in reach, or the whiff stun), action "tackle_stand" with the coded stab pose (`tackle_stand_seconds` 0.35; no standing tackle clip exists), and slides only with `ai.json` `slide_tackle_chance` 0.15.
- Ball won or missed the moment it comes in reach during the slide (not only at the press); a slide that never reaches the ball ends with the whiff stun. The user accepted the risk of missing (fouls do not exist yet). Reach, chance and stuns are as before.
- TACKLE: in reach (`tackle_reach` 1.3 m) a chance to knock the ball loose (`tackle_success` 0.55, from tackling and the carrier's balance). A miss slows the tackler. Tackling when nobody has the ball does not stun. A keeper with the ball in his HANDS cannot be tackled; with the ball at his feet he can be tackled anywhere, even in his area (`TackleRules.is_protected` = the carrier has the ball in his hands; before: any keeper inside his area).
- Shoulder charge: double-tap the joystick when your side does not have the ball (keyboard F). Within `shoulder_reach` 1.4 m: win and the carrier is pushed, off balance, cannot retake the ball for a moment and the ball comes loose; lose and the charger bounces off and is slowed. Too far: a small lunge and a short slowdown. Decided by strength (plus a little balance): chance = 0.5 + strength difference x 0.01, clamped 0.15 to 0.85. A keeper with the ball in his hands is protected.
- Automatic switch: when defending, control jumps to a teammate who is clearly closer. SWITCH jumps to the teammate nearest the ball.
- Balls won (tackles, shoulder charges, steals) are counted in the match stats.

## Where it lives
- Code: `scripts/gameplay/press_assist.gd` (PressAssist): `apply`, `pull` (stick moved). `scripts/gameplay/match_up_assist.gd` (MatchUpAssist): `apply`. `scripts/gameplay/tackle_rules.gd` (TackleRules): `attempt`, `success_chance`, `is_protected`. `scripts/gameplay/shoulder_charge.gd` (ShoulderCharge): `attempt`, `success_chance`, `duel_power`.
- Code: `scripts/gameplay/human_assists.gd` (HumanAssists): `apply` (PRESS with or without the stick).
- Code: `scripts/gameplay/human_control.gd` (HumanControl, `is_attacking` switches the layout). `scripts/ui/floating_stick.gd` (FloatingStick) detects the double tap.
- Numbers: `data/config/player.json` -> `press_*` (`press_close_distance` 2.0, `press_goal_side_offset` 0.6, `press_tackle_reach_factor` 0.85, `press_tackle_interval` 0.6, `press_auto_tackle` 0, `press_stick_pull` 0.25; the three tackle ones only matter when `press_auto_tackle` is 1); `match_up_*` (`match_up_contain_distance` 1.6, `match_up_lead_seconds` 0.25, ...); `tackle_*` (`tackle_reach` 1.3, `tackle_success` 0.55, `tackle_fail_stun` 0.6, `tackle_whiff_stun` 0.35, `tackle_cooldown` 0.8, ...); `shoulder_*` (`shoulder_reach` 1.4, `shoulder_cooldown` 1.0, `shoulder_base_chance` 0.5, `shoulder_strength_factor` 0.01, `shoulder_min_chance` 0.15, `shoulder_max_chance` 0.85, `shoulder_victim_stun` 0.5, ...); `default_strength` 50.
- Numbers: `data/config/stat_effects.json` -> `tackle_success`, `tackle_resist` (balance), `shoulder_balance_weight` 0.3, `intercept_radius`; AI: `mark_distance`, `tackle_attempt_mult`.
- Numbers: `data/config/controls.json` -> `double_tap_seconds` 0.3, `double_tap_distance`, `tap_max_seconds`.
- Tests: `tests/physics_smoke_test.gd`: "shoulder charge: about half the duels won at equal strength", "shoulder charge: out of reach keeps the ball with the carrier", "shoulder charge: the stronger player has more chance", steal checks. `tests/stats_test.tscn`: tackling and balance, strength in shoulder duels.

## Do not undo
- PRESS is the SPRINT button when your team does not have the ball (eFootball-style "Dash & Pressure", user request), and the selected player goes to take the ball by himself. Do not move it back to SHOOT.
- PRESS only brings the player to the ball; the tackle happens when the user clicks TACKLE (user request, 2026-11-15). Keep `press_auto_tackle` 0.
- PRESS must work on a loose ball: it shows unless the loose ball is our own pass on its way (user report, 2026-11-15).
- PRESS with the joystick moved follows the stick with a small pull toward the press (`press_stick_pull` 0.25, user wish 2026-11-17; before, a plain sprint where it points, 2026-11-15); the stick still leads, it is only "a little difficult" to pull away. PRESS alone is the automatic press.
- Shoulder charge on a joystick double tap, decided by strength (and a little balance), as the user asked. Do not make it a fixed chance.
- Labels and meaning of the four buttons switch with possession (`HumanControl.is_attacking`); SWITCH is the PASS button on defense.
- A keeper with the ball in his hands cannot be tackled or shoulder-charged; with the ball at his feet he can (user decision, 2026-11-15; before, any keeper in his area was protected).
- MATCH-UP does not auto-tackle (tap TACKLE).
- TACKLE always shows the slide, even with no ball to win (user request, 2026-11-16). The slide can miss; the user accepted this until fouls exist.

- The AI tackles standing and slides rarely (`slide_tackle_chance` 0.15); the human's TACKLE is always a slide (user, 2026-11-16).
